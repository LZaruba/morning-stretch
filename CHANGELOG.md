## 1.0.1

First release, for Minecraft 26.3.

- Stretch animation after waking up in a bed and on a key press (R by default)
- First and third person poses, breathing and yawning sounds
- Nearby players see the animation in multiplayer
- While stretching: walk at sneaking speed, no sprinting
- While stretching: hidden name tag, hidden from the locator bar, not found by commands or command suggestions
