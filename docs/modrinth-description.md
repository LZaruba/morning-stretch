**Morning Stretch** lets your character stretch: arms flung wide and up, a slight lean back and a big yawn, just like after waking up.

## Features

- **Wake-up stretch**: your character stretches automatically after sleeping in a bed.
- **Stretch any time**: press **R** (rebindable under *Controls → Miscellaneous*).
- **Multiplayer**: nearby players see you stretch.
- **Sounds**: breathing and a yawn play along with the animation.
- Works in **first and third person**.

## While you stretch

For about four seconds:

- you walk at **sneaking speed** and cannot sprint,
- other players **do not see your name tag** or your **dot on the locator bar**,
- **commands cannot find you** (`@a`, `@p`, `@r`, `@e` or your name), and your name is not offered in command suggestions.

## Installation

Requires [Fabric API](https://modrinth.com/mod/fabric-api).

Install it on **both the client and the server** for the full experience. It also works on one side only:

- **client only**: you can stretch, but only you see it,
- **server only**: players stretch after waking up and are hidden from the locator bar and from commands, but nobody sees the animation.
